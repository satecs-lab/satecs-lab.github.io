초록

SAR systems provide wide-area images regardless of weather and time of day, but wideband SAR is vulnerable to interference from adjacent-band devices, causing image distortion. A SAR point-target simulator is developed to analyze distortion caused by narrowband and wideband interference received from adjacent bands, evaluating response width, peak sidelobe ratio and integrated sidelobe ratio as JSR varies. Simulation results are consistent with interference phenomena observed in actual SAR satellite imagery.
