초록

We show that an L-branch maximal ratio combining (MRC) receiver with an average signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) γ̅ in Nakagami-m fading channels is equivalent to an mL-branch MRC receiver with average SNR γ̅/m in Rayleigh fading channels in terms of symbol error probability (SEP) when mL is an integer. Moreover, the orthogonal space-time block code (OSTBC) employing Mttransmit antennas and Mrreceive antennas with the average SNR γ̅ in Nakagami-m fading channels is shown to be equivalent to an mMtMr-branch MRC single-input-multiple-output (SIMO) receiver with the average SNR γ̅/mMtin Rayleigh fading channels.
