초록

SAR uses radio waves to detect targets in all-weather conditions, and recent developments enable drone-based SAR systems to detect low-RCS targets hidden in vegetation. In drone SAR, weight constraints limit battery capacity and pulse sampling, degrading image quality. This study enhances the image quality of power-limited drone SAR by applying compressive sensing algorithms. Field experiments demonstrate adaptive detection of low-RCS concealed targets, showing that drone SAR can effectively obtain images of buried or vegetation-hidden targets with the enhanced algorithm.
